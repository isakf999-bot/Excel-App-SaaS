# Nordic Ledger

A custom theme for Stadga: established Nordic finance with a quiet wellness undertone. Built from Forest Canopy structure and Modern Minimalist restraint.

## Color Palette

- **Ink**: `#1a1f1c` - Primary text
- **Forest**: `#2d4a2b` - Brand and approved states
- **Sage**: `#5c6b58` - Secondary text
- **Paper**: `#f6f3ec` - Application background
- **Cream**: `#fbfaf6` - Surfaces
- **Reject**: `#8b3a2f` - Denied amounts
- **Review**: `#8a6320` - Needs human judgment

## Typography

- **Headers**: Instrument Serif
- **Body Text**: IBM Plex Sans
- **Figures**: IBM Plex Mono

## Best Used For

Expense compliance, payroll-adjacent tools, HR finance, Swedish B2B products that should feel older than a startup.

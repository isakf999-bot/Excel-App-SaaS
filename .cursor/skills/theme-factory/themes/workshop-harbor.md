# Workshop Harbor

Custom ReviewFlow identity. Warm workshop paper, harbor navy, and a craft serif — trustworthy for local trades, polished enough for SaaS.

## Color Palette

- **Linen**: `#F4F1EA` - Page background
- **Paper**: `#FFFCF7` - Cards and raised surfaces
- **Ink**: `#1B1914` - Headlines and primary text
- **Slate**: `#5E594F` - Body and muted copy
- **Harbor**: `#1F3A5F` - Primary accent, used sparingly
- **Harbor soft**: `#E6EDF5` - Soft accent fills
- **Line**: `#DDD6C8` - Hairline borders
- **Sage**: `#2F6B52` - Success / completed states
- **Star**: `#C9A227` - Review stars

## Typography

- **Headers**: Fraunces
- **Body Text**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Figures**: Plus Jakarta Sans (tabular)

## Best Used For

Local-service SaaS, review follow-up products, and tools that should feel useful rather than flashy.

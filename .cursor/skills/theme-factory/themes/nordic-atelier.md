# Nordic Atelier

A darker, more editorial sibling of Nordic Ledger. Same Swedish institutional bones — serif headlines, tracked kickers, thin rules — shifted into a warm charcoal studio.

## Color Palette

- **Paper**: `#12100e` - Application background
- **Cream**: `#1a1714` - Surfaces and cards
- **Ink**: `#ebe4d6` - Primary text (warm paper)
- **Ink soft**: `#c4b8a4` - Secondary text
- **Sage**: `#9a9184` - Muted labels
- **Olive**: `#8a9274` - Brand and approved states
- **Brass**: `#b08a4a` - Sparse luxury accent
- **Line**: `#2c2822` - Dividers
- **Reject**: `#c46a5a` - Errors and denied states
- **Review**: `#c4a05a` - Needs attention

## Typography

- **Headers**: Source Serif 4
- **Body Text**: Source Sans 3
- **Figures**: IBM Plex Mono

## Best Used For

Premium Nordic creative tools, e-commerce studios, and products that should feel older than a startup — but after dusk.
